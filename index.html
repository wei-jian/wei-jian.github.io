About Me

I obtained my Dr. rer. nat. in Informatics from Eberhard Karls Universität Tübingen, Germany. Currently, I am an Associate Professor, Doctoral & Master Supervisor, and Head of the Electronic Engineering Division in the College of Communication Engineering, Jilin University. My research focuses on stereoscopic perception and intelligent recognition for digital vision, including 3D computer vision, autostereoscopic light-field display, environmental perception, and pattern recognition. I serve as PI of 12 research projects including NSFC General/Youth Programs, Jilin Provincial Science and Technology Projects, and industrial R&D projects with aerospace and aviation institutes, and participate in national key R&D programs and medical-engineering interdisciplinary research as a core team member. I am a recipient of the “Lixin Outstanding Young Faculty Fellowship” of Jilin University, Senior Member of CCF, Member of CSIG, Committee Member of CSIG Technical Committees on Image Application & System Integration and 3D Imaging & Display, Director of Jilin Society for Image and Graphics, and Editorial Board Member of Journal of Imaging and Journal of China Universities of Posts and Telecommunications (English Edition). I have published more than 70 peer-reviewed papers, been granted over 20 Chinese invention patents and 1 US patent, and was awarded the First Prize of Jilin Provincial Technological Invention Award (5/14).

Research Interests

  • 1. Intelligent 3D Reconstruction with Multi-View Geometry
    Targeting metaverse, digital twin and autonomous driving perception, we study multi-view stereo matching, dynamic dense reconstruction and occlusion-aware depth estimation, to tackle challenges under textureless regions, highly dynamic scenes and complex illumination, and build high-precision 3D reconstruction pipelines for intelligent perception and digital twin infrastructure.
  • 2. Light-Field Video and Autostereoscopic 3D Display Systems
    For immersive interaction and next-generation display industry upgrading, we investigate light-field capture, light-field video prediction, multi-view fusion and autostereoscopic rendering, to overcome bottlenecks of immersion, resolution and viewing angle, and build integrated sensing-computing-rendering-display light-field systems for metaverse, medical visualization and intelligent cockpit applications.
  • 3. Endoscopic 3D Perception for Precision Medicine
    Minimally invasive surgery and smart healthcare motivate our work on highlight-suppressed monocular depth estimation, intraoperative tissue analysis and medical imaging CAD. We address highlight artifacts and complex micro-anatomy in endoscopy, and develop endoscopic 3D perception systems to support precise minimally invasive treatment and intelligent surgical navigation.
  • 4. Intelligent Analysis of Space-Borne Remote Sensing Data
    For national strategic demands on space information sensing, we focus on SAR and optical multi-source remote sensing interpretation, spatial modeling, target recognition and geolocation, to break processing bottlenecks in complex scenes and build space-ground integrated analysis capacity for national security, resource survey and geospatial industry.
  • 5. Computational Camera Vision for Emerging Industries
    Towards human-computer interaction, industrial intelligence and digital education, we study video prediction, few-shot view synthesis, saliency detection and affective computing, and build general efficient visual intelligence frameworks for industrial inspection, intelligent surveillance, human-AI collaboration and digital education transformation.
